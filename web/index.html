Scenario assumptions

Mixed mode starts in baseline and uses illustrative monthly transitions: from baseline, 1.2% to recession, 0.4% to persistent stress, 0.2% to sudden shock. Crisis states introduce shifts and a shared Student-t (5 degrees of freedom) shock. These are not estimated event probabilities. The underlying bootstrap may already contain extreme months; overlays can double-count stress. Forced scenarios begin at the forecast start, not at a predicted event date.
